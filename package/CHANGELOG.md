# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.

- Independent hands: each grabs, holds an item and has its own grip.
- White Knuckle hanging, swinging and grab jumps; anything climbable in PEAK is a hold, plus ropes, vines, chains
  and pitons.
- Grip rings per hand, coloured like PEAK's stamina bar.
- Rain, wind, special surfaces, thorns and poison vines affect holds.
- White Knuckle-style inventory: pockets on Q / E, drop and throw on 1 / 3, swap on 2, both hands can eat and throw
  at once, feeding teammates by looking at them.
- Floating backpack slots for packs on the ground, in your hand and on teammates' backs; rocket pack lighting and
  jetpack refuelling.
- Carrying knocked-out scouts with hold Tab; grabbing, dragging and pulling up teammates.
- Gamepad support with White Knuckle's layout; Peakle's controls are rebound in Mod Settings, PEAK's Controls menu stays vanilla.
- Gameplay settings come from the host in multiplayer.
